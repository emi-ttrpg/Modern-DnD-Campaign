A vampire of some kind working under [[Kanaan Kasandra]]. He was described holding a strange orb that upon breaking released a wraith that transformed the things it killed into purple specters. He wore red gloves with a full suit and tie, he obscured his face and carried a large parasol to block sunlight. Before the orb was shattered, it appeared to allow Eric control over several Orcs that he had thralled. During this confrontation Eric made a few references to someone who "...keeps secrets" and advised Scott to "...mind (his) secrets and (his) friends...". Whether this was in reference to Kanaan Kassandra, or [[Vecna|someone more powerful]], is unclear.

Recently Eric paid a house visit to Dominique's home while we were all camped out there. He requested we give him what his employer looking for. We played dumb but he made it clear he was looking for Charlotte. Eric, being a vampire, was likely only allowed entry due to the doormat welcoming him in outside of the home. That being said, it is possible that Dominique's [[Welcome Home - Home Security|Welcome Home Security]] branded doorbell also would have allowed Eric the same access even if there were no doormat.
