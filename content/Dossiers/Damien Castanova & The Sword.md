The youngest sibling of [[The Castanova Family]], Damien has been intentionally kept from the realities of what his family does. His siblings all seem to agree it is best to keep Damien as far away from the "family business" as possible in order to protect "the inheritance".

While his siblings are unpleased with Damien's recent involvement in affairs regarding Charlotte and his attempts to look into Kanaan Kasandra, they are hesitant if not totally unwilling to disclose why. It was mentioned that the one in charge of the inheritance wasn't "...doing good protecting it." and it's safe to assume Damien's recent run-ins with the police and other danger may be why.

The file we grabbed from Alin was a personal file on Damien, it referenced how sick he was as a child. Constantly in and out of the hospital. These medical records reached from pediatric files, to recent medical reports showing a rapid recovery of health over the last two years. It's referenced that these sudden improvements could be "symptoms of the inheritance".

During a spell, we discovered that there's a soul seemingly bound throughout Damien's body.
