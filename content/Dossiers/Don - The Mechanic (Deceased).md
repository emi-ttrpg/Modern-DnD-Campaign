Owner of [[Modren Mechanics]], Don helped the party with various small issues and then requested our help when a band of [[The Goblins - Worshippers of Maglubiyet|The Goblins - Worshippers of Maglubiyet]] attacked his shop in an attempt to steal an arcane drive. This drive was in a car he referred to as [[Charlotte]].

Later, the party discovered Charlotte wasn't the car, but the drive. Looking at logs Don kept about his work, we discovered that he was having conversations with someone, possibly Charlotte, and that he ensured the radio was the first compatible piece of tech in every vehicle she was installed into. Don was presumably employed by [[Kanaan Kasandra]], as in conversation Mr. Kasandra explained that if we gave him what he was looking for this could all be over with.
