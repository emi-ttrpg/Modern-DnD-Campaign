One of the CEO's of [[Fantasy Boeing]], The General worked on increasingly militaristic projects using the company's assets until the public began to find his actions strange given his current standing as a General. The most prevalent projects for these contracts were ones in the field of robotics. [[Kanaan Kasandra]] used to work for the company while The General was CEO and it's where he got his start.

Information accessed on the internet tells us:
- He is an older [[Scott SC-077|4-star General]]
- The company sponsored racer [[Francis Denton]] while he was CEO

During the summit, Scott and Cain broke into a temporary back office. Inside the office was a laptop we figured belonged to the general. Searching revealed a clue to the passcode, "My most important discovery." Upon entering the word "[[Vecna|eye]]", the laptop unlocked. Quickly files and logs regarding [[Scott SC-077|"Soldier Constructs"]] were accessed.

Personal ramblings of the general had phrases such as, "The eye has spoken to me..." and discuss that it has revealed to The General a way to access magic without will. To use magic, a being needs a soul to access the weave. These files described a method of stripping a soul of it's important data marks. What's left behind, is a soul lacking memory, personality, and only the power necessary to act as a battery and conduit for the weave.
