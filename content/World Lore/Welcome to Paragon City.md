Paragon city, previously known as Baulders Gate, is a member of the [[S.C.C.S]]. In Paragon city and the entirety of the the [[S.C.C.S]] have one major rule, no individual use of magic. While occasionally used in the development of technologies, use of magic by individuals is shunned and highly illegal. Enforced by the [[H.E.X.]]. which poses tools to detect the use of high level magic from a distance, but anything even residual at a closer range.
