Neverwinter was reportedly destroyed by a nuclear core meltdown roughly 50 years ago. The city still suffers from the arcane necrotic fallout. Occasionally reports of unusual activity from the abandoned city make their way to the ears of the citizens of Paragon and the rest of the functioning [[S.C.C.S]]. [[Kanaan Kasandra]] gave the party a pamphlet advertising the city from roughly 50 years ago prior to it's destruction, he encouraged us to visit some time.
