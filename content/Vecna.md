Not much is known by us in world, but we believe that [[Kanaan Kasandra]] posses the hand, and [[The General]] possess the eye.

The party encountered some of Vecna's cultists on an oil rig off the coast of the S.C.C.S. The leader of the branch possessed a cape with the symbol of Vecna on the back in golden stitching. Thanks to Ava's avid internet usage, the party was able to identify the symbol. The ring at the bottom representing the eye and the top representing the fingers of his hand.

The party recently encountered anther worshipper of Vecna, a mysterious woman who easily destroyed "secrets" and eviscerated a horde of Kassandra's Vampiric thralls. She mentioned that "...he acts as if he is in control, but he is not." whether that's referring to The General or Kanaan is unclear.
