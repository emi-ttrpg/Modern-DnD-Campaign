Originally a general engineering contracting company, when [[The General]] became CEO, the company shifted to private use government contracting and focused heavily on robotics. In the eyes of the public this was seen as a conflict of interests, given The General's active military status and position. No one is sure what happened to the CEO who preceded The General. [[Kanaan Kasandra]] worked for the company for a time, and it's largely thought this is where he got his start in engineering.
